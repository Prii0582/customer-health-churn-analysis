How the health score works

Usage trend (30 pts)Sessions in the last 3 months vs the 3 months before: +5% or better = 30, -5% to +5% = 24, -15% to -5% = 15, -30% to -15% = 6, worse than -30% = 0
Active days (25 pts)Average days per month the customer logged in (last 3 months): 18+ = 25, 14-17 = 18, 8-13 = 10, 1-7 = 4, none = 0
Modules adopted (25 pts)Modules used on 8+ days in the latest month, out of 6: 25 x (modules / 6)
Open tickets (20 pts)Unresolved support tickets: 0 = 20, 1 = 14, 2 = 8, 3+ = 0; minus 5 if any open ticket is High priority
Risk bandCritical below 40, High 40-59, Medium 60-79, Healthy 80+. A renewal within 3 months moves a non-healthy customer up one risk level.
